## Signed-in Support

Open **Support** from Settings → Account or the workspace menu.

1. Choose New request and the relevant type: support, general question, feedback,
   custom development, grievance or security report, as offered by the current form.
2. Give it a short subject and explain the issue or requirement.
3. Submit once and keep the request reference.
4. Open the request to read its conversation and send a follow-up.

Requests belong to your account. The list is paged; changing its page does not
close an active request. Reload the list/detail when checking for an update.
Support is not a live chat and has no implied immediate reply time; current
support hours and contact email are published on [Contact](/contact/).

## Make an issue reproducible

Include the affected page, approximate date/time and timezone, record ID, what
you expected and what actually happened. For a trading simulation, include the
instrument, side/order type, relevant price or rule and session/order/backtest ID.
Quote the visible error text when possible.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery links, cookies, API keys, broker
secrets or another person's data. Describe a security issue without destructive
testing. The current ticket form accepts text rather than a general file upload.

## Contact before you have an account

Use [Contact](/contact/) or [Feedback](/feedback/), complete the offered verification
and retain the receipt/reference. A receipt is not an anonymous link to read a
private conversation.

If you later create an account with the same verified email, eligible earlier
guest requests can be associated when you sign in or refresh access. An unverified
email alone does not claim someone else's requests. Use Support if you cannot
locate the earlier conversation.

## Replies, status and email

Your follow-up normally changes a request to Awaiting support. A staff reply
normally changes it to Awaiting customer. Other statuses show Received,
In progress, Resolved or Closed. A follow-up can reopen the conversation for
support; a status label is not a guaranteed completion date.

Account/support email sends acknowledgements and reply notifications through the
configured mail service. Check spam and your registered address when a message
is missing. **Reply inside the Support page:** an ordinary reply to the email is
not automatically imported into the ticket conversation.

If a send or reload appears to fail, check whether the request/reply was saved
before submitting the same text again. Email delivery and saved in-app replies
are separate outcomes.

## Custom Development requests

Choose Custom Development and describe the inputs, desired outputs and who will
use the software. No QuantNest subscription or Private purchase is needed to
make an enquiry.

Only the initial request and support replies belong to this platform. Project
scoping, agreements, payments, delivery, source ownership and continuing project
communication are agreed separately. There is no in-platform project workspace,
milestone tracker or project-payment checkout.

## Billing, privacy and account questions

Use [Billing](/docs/billing/) for payment/refund status and [Account controls](/docs/controls/)
for export/deactivation/identity erasure. Mention the relevant reference when
contacting Support. A ticket does not cancel subscription renewal, close a trade
or change a running strategy merely because you described that intention.
