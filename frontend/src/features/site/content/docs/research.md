## Start a conversation

- Ask a general question without choosing stocks first.
- For stock analysis, select supported instruments or search your Terminal watchlists by name and choose a timeframe. The selected watchlist is retained with the conversation.
- Attach your strategy or completed backtests within your request allowance when the question concerns your own work.

## Keep the right context

- Attachment chips show what is included. Remove a chip to exclude it from the next question.
- Follow up in the same conversation to refine an idea or revise a draft.
- Add resolved stocks to the next request without replacing stocks already selected.
- Reloading restores saved work. Refreshing an analysis creates a new observation rather than changing the date of an old result.
- Opening a conversation shows its latest reply. Scroll up to read earlier turns without being pulled back down by updates.

## Inspect the answer

- Read the conclusion, result cards and limitations.
- Open citations to inspect supporting evidence and observation times.
- A quote and a completed candle are different observations. Check which one supports the answer.
- AI can be wrong. Missing evidence is a reason to investigate further, not proof of a trading opportunity.

## Confirm proposed changes

- The assistant can propose a draft strategy, a watchlist change or a backtest. For watchlist additions, choose the named destination and review it before confirming.
- Read the preview before confirming. Backtests show the rules, dates, capital, costs and slippage.
- Each new experiment needs its own confirmation. Asking a question does not authorise autonomous trading.

## Progress and usage

- One research turn can run at a time. Follow its progress or use Cancel while it is active.
- The composer shows remaining AI credits; completed replies show credits consumed. One credit equals 1,000 reported AI tokens.
- Explore includes 150 credits, Build 500 and Pro 1,500 per allowance period. Your current plan and balance are shown in Billing.
- Only a successfully saved answer consumes credits. Failed or cancelled requests do not consume customer credits.
- An accepted answer can finish even if it uses the remaining balance. New questions wait until credits are available again; renewal starts with a fresh allowance and no carried debt.
- Screening uses its separate run allowance. Development records AI usage without enforcing a quota; Private has no commercial quota.
- Earlier saved conversation content remains available after an error. Inspect the affected message and retry if useful.
