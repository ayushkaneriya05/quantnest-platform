## What Private includes

QuantNest Private is available on request with a direct quotation. QuantNest
manages a separate deployment for your agreed users. It includes research,
screening, Strategy Studio, saved versions, backtesting, Monte Carlo, manual
Terminal simulation, automated Paper, journal, reports and account controls,
plus supported broker connections and automated Live strategy execution.

Explore, Build and Pro are public research/simulation plans. Purchasing one does
not activate Live or move your records into a Private deployment. Compare the
current [features and allowances](/pricing/) before choosing your workspace.

**Terminal is simulated in every offering.** Real broker orders come from
Private's [Live strategy deployments](/docs/live/).

## A managed deployment

Your instance has its own hostname, database, credentials and backups. You sign
in through the ordinary QuantNest interface. Registration, security and account
controls use the shared application; there is no separate Private sign-in flow.

QuantNest operates the server. Source-code delivery and server administration
access are not included by default. Hosting, updates, backup retention, recovery,
support hours and any uptime commitments belong in the written agreement.
Managing the deployment does not mean choosing trades or managing your funds.

Your public account, saved work, payments and broker connections are not
automatically copied. Agree any required data transfer during onboarding.

## Pricing and capacity

Private has contact pricing rather than a fixed monthly subscription amount.
The quotation identifies setup, the service period, server capacity, hosting,
support and any third-party data or AI costs. Additional requirements are agreed
before they become part of the service.

There are **no public-plan quota ceilings** in a Private instance. This does not
mean unlimited CPU, provider API access or free AI tokens. Technical request
controls, account funds, instrument validity and operational limits still apply.

Quotes, agreements, payments, renewals and payment records are handled directly.
There is no in-app Private payment checkout, automatic subscription or
software-enforced expiry. The public first-purchase refund workflow does not
govern the Private agreement; discuss its cancellation and refund terms directly.

## Onboarding step by step

1. [Send a Private enquiry](/contact/?type=private) with your broker, markets,
   intended sessions/instruments, users, timing and support needs. Do not send keys.
2. Review the proposed scope, costs, service period and operating responsibilities.
3. QuantNest prepares the deployment and gives you its workspace address.
4. Create/protect your instance-local account and save the strategy version to use.
5. Complete [broker authorization](/docs/brokers/) in the workspace. FYERS is
   currently implemented; other broker integrations are not delivered yet.
6. Confirm data readiness and verify your intended workflow with an explicitly
   reviewed test before relying on automated Live execution.

## Daily use and renewal

Keep broker authorization current. Inspect connection readiness, applied session
state, orders and positions. Read the [Live guide](/docs/live/) before using pause,
version changes or Stop & close. Broker acceptance/fills remain different from
local signals, and Live reports currently exclude actual broker charges.

Contact Support about changes, renewal or offboarding before the agreed period
ends. Coordinate pending orders, open exposure, records and access with QuantNest.
No automated expiry process liquidates positions or renews your agreement.

Custom Development remains a [separate project service](/custom-development/);
buying Private does not automatically purchase custom development or source rights.
