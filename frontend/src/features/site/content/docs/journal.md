## Choose the right records

- Select Terminal, Paper or available Private Live records.
- Set dates and inspect account context and timestamps.
- Partial exits are separate recorded closes.

## Save a trade review

- Record the setup, decision context, lessons and mistake tags.
- Add a self-reported execution rating when useful.
- Notes are private; they are included in research only when you select that option.

## Read Reports

- Inspect realised P&L, charges and available breakdowns for the chosen source and dates.
- Historical equity ratios and account drawdown require a verified equity series and may be unavailable.
- Review ratings describe your own assessment, not an objective performance score.

## Investigate a result

- Use Ask AI from a trade review or report to prepare a conversation with the relevant records.
- Ask a specific question and inspect the evidence supporting the answer.
- A missing record cannot establish the cause of an outcome.
