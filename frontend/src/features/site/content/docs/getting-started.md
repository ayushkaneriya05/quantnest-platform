## Create and protect your account

- Register, verify your email and sign in.
- Open Profile → Security, enable two-factor authentication and keep recovery codes private.
- Reopen this guide from the workspace profile menu whenever you need it.

## Choose your first task

- Ask the [research assistant](/docs/research/) a general question or attach selected stocks.
- If you already have a trading idea, go to the [strategy builder](/docs/builder/) and define its conditions.
- The initial supported strategy workflow is for NSE cash stocks. Public subscriptions do not place real broker orders.

## Define and save your rules

- Choose instruments, timeframe, entry/exit conditions, sizing and restrictions.
- Save a version with a note describing your idea. Saving does not deploy it.
- Review the saved configuration before testing.

## Run a historical test

- Select dates, capital, slippage and a charge profile.
- Review the workload estimate and credit requirement, then confirm.
- Inspect trades, costs, diagnostics and coverage alongside the headline metrics. Read the [simulation assumptions](/methodology/).

## Practise and review

- Use [Terminal](/docs/terminal/) for manual simulation or [Paper](/docs/paper/) to run your saved rules automatically.
- Keep their records separate. Add private notes in [Journal](/docs/journal/) and review the relevant Reports.
- Check Billing and Usage for remaining allowances before starting more work.
