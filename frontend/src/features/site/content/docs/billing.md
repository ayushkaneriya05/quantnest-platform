## Before payment

- Sign in with a verified account.
- Review the plan, allowances, price, tax and total shown at checkout.
- Automatic monthly renewal requires consent. Use manual payment when available if you do not want to authorise a mandate.

## Payment status

- Activation follows payment verification; a browser callback alone is not proof of a captured payment.
- If processing is delayed, check Billing and Usage before trying to pay again.
- Your confirmed period, invoices and payment status appear in the account.

## Allowances

- Explore, Build and Pro currently support NSE stocks.
- NSE futures and options are coming soon in Build and Pro. They are not available today, and no release date is announced. Upgrading adds capacity for the currently supported workflow.
- Live trading is supported through an activated Private installation with an agreed supported broker setup, rather than a public subscription.

- Explore resets each calendar month in Asia/Kolkata. Paid allowances follow the verified billing period.
- Used and reserved work are shown separately; a running job stays associated with the period in which it started.
- Archived but undeleted strategies count as saved inventory. Paused Paper deployments count as active capacity.
- There is no rollover or automatic paid overage.

## Renewal and cancellation

- Cancel future renewal while keeping the current paid period.
- Plan changes are initially scheduled at renewal.
- Saved results remain available when a limit is exhausted. Further work must fit your current plan.
- A paid public plan does not activate Live execution.

## Refund requests

- The first paid Workspace purchase has a seven-day refund window alongside applicable remedies.
- Submit your payment reference and reason, then track the request status and relevant credit note.
- Read the [refund policy](/refunds/) for Private and custom-work distinctions.
