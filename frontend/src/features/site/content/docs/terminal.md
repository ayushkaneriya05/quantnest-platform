## Inspect a stock

- Search a supported instrument and add it to your watchlist.
- Open its chart, choose an interval and inspect candle dates and times.
- Quotes are separately timestamped observations; check their time before using them.

## Practise an order

- Choose side, quantity, order type and relevant prices.
- Review the ticket before confirming the simulated order.
- Inspect status changes. Modify or cancel eligible pending orders when required.
- Orders are evaluated against available observed-price data, not a real exchange orderbook.

## Review your practice

- Follow Terminal positions, close history and account summaries.
- Select Terminal in Journal and Reports so manual practice stays separate from automated Paper.
- Partial closes are separate records. Some charge/equity metrics may be unavailable.

## Understand the boundary

- Terminal does not place real broker orders.
- It cannot reproduce exchange depth, queue position, every partial fill or broker latency.
- Protective controls depend on functioning services and current observations.
