## 3. How the employer research was checked

The search covered official employer sites, linked applicant-tracking systems, employer job posts and selected secondary listings across trading firms, brokers, exchange infrastructure, financial-data companies, banks and fintech vendors.

**This is a broad public-source map, not a claim to enumerate every company or unadvertised vacancy in the region.** There is no complete public feed linking every local employer to its current hiring team, technologies and graduate eligibility. Private referrals and campus-only hiring will be missing.

Use these status labels:

| Label | Meaning | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Listed** | A role is visible on the employer's site or official recruiting system | That applications remain unfilled or that final-year applicants qualify |
| **Open approach** | The employer accepts CVs/internship enquiries, but no matching junior vacancy was confirmed | A guaranteed opening |
| **Verify** | Secondary listing, conflicting location, inaccessible live page, or uncertain current status | A confirmed active vacancy |
| **Watch** | Relevant business or local office; no suitable current local role established | Local quant hiring |
| **Historical/outside area** | Old, closed, or located outside the requested region | A current local opportunity |

Undated careers pages can remain online after hiring ends. Reopen the linked job before applying, record its title/ID, and verify location and graduation eligibility. Research describes **published role requirements**, not confidential internal trading models, profitability, working conditions or the quality of a firm's engineering.

## 10. Interview preparation by role

The exercises below are my preparation recommendations. They are not claimed leaked or guaranteed interview questions from these employers.

### Python / trading developer

Be able to explain and implement:

- Maintaining an incremental rolling statistic and comparing it with batch calculation.

- Why a fast calculation can still have slow end-to-end response time.

### SQL / financial-data engineer

Practice with `accounts`, `orders`, `fills`, `positions`, `prices` and `daily_returns` tables:

1. Obtain the latest valid event for each order using an explicit tie-breaker.
2. Compute weighted average fill price without double-counting duplicates.
3. Identify positions whose quantity disagrees with accepted fills.
4. Calculate daily net P&L by account with a clearly stated timezone.
5. Join a signal to the latest price known before that signal, never a later one.
6. Find gaps and duplicate timestamps in a candle series.
7. Explain an index and verify its effect with a query plan.
8. Explain transaction isolation and simultaneous updates to account funds.

Do not rely on SQL syntax memorization alone; explain the financial meaning of each query.

### Quantitative analyst / researcher

Practice these until you can reason aloud without a prepared notebook:

- Expected value and variance; conditioning and Bayes' rule.
- Correlation versus causation; why highly correlated strategies may still behave differently in stress.
- Regression assumptions, residuals, overfitting and regularization.
- Why randomly splitting dependent market observations can mislead.
- Why trying many variants changes how convincing the best result is.
- Sharpe ratio assumptions, drawdown, turnover and transaction costs.
- The distinction between predictive accuracy and a useful trading decision.
- Missing observations, survivorship, corporate actions and feature availability time.
- Why a profitable backtest may not survive execution constraints.
- How to report a result that does not support the original hypothesis.

For InRisk, add expected payout, threshold uncertainty and basis risk: a trigger-based payment can differ from the customer's actual loss. For trading research, add spread, market depth, impact and the difference between a candle and the actual event sequence.

### Fintech / AI engineer

Prepare API validation, authentication/authorization, retries, idempotency, provider timeouts, streaming, structured outputs and evaluation. Explain the difference between selecting a tool, requesting an action and executing an authorized persistent change.

For ML positions, also show a trained/evaluated baseline and explain the target, loss, split, metrics and deployment monitoring. Calling a hosted model is useful software work, but it does not by itself establish statistical modeling skill.

### C++ stretch track

Start with references/pointers, lifetime, RAII, smart pointers, STL containers, iterator invalidation, const correctness, move semantics, basic threads and debugging. Then implement and profile one small deterministic program. Do not begin with lock-free algorithms or claim microsecond guarantees before you can explain ownership and correctness.

## 17. A simple application tracker

Keep this in a spreadsheet or private note; no new application is needed:

```text
Company | Exact role | Job ID | Official URL | Actual location
Source status | Date checked | Experience requirement | 2027 eligible?
CV variant | Relevant artifact | Applied date | Contact/referral
Response | Next action | Follow-up date | Interview gaps | Offer details
```

Record “unknown” rather than guessing. Keep one record per requisition; do not count the same job on LinkedIn, an aggregator and the employer portal three times. Recheck open-approach leads weekly, not daily.

Useful saved search phrases include:

- `Python trading developer Ahmedabad`, `quantitative analyst GIFT City`, `quant intern Gandhinagar`.
- `financial data analyst Ahmedabad`, `fintech backend intern Ahmedabad`, `Python SQL internship GIFT City`.
- `trading support Python Linux Gandhinagar`, `2027 data engineer apprentice India`.

Always move from a search result to the actual employer/ATS listing. Search-engine snippets can retain withdrawn jobs; the Bank of America example demonstrates this directly.

## 18. Choosing between offers or widening the search

Score an actual opportunity on relevant coding/research work, mentorship, pay, manageable location/hours and future skill growth. Treat the scoring as your preference, not a scientific ranking.

For your stated direction, a supervised Python/data internship with real code ownership can be more useful than a “quant” title with no analytical work. Since you have already completed an internship in a production ERP codebase, prefer a next role that adds ownership, financial-data experience or strong mentorship rather than repeating introductory training. Conversely, a strong trading-support team can teach market mechanics and provide automation opportunities; ask for those responsibilities rather than assuming a later transfer.

If local eligibility blocks most applications after several weeks of targeted effort, widen in this order:

1. Local Python/data engineering internships outside pure quant, with financial or transaction-heavy work preferred.
2. Remote/hybrid research engineering where the employer explicitly permits your location.
3. Other Indian finance/technology hubs, if you decide relocation is acceptable.

Do not silently mark multi-location roles as local to improve the apparent number of opportunities. The location constraint meaningfully narrows the current search, especially for pure quantitative research and C++ trading infrastructure.
